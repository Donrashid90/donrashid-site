The sound

What is West Coast G-Funk?

G-Funk is a style of hip-hop associated with the US West Coast. It is known for funk-influenced basslines, relaxed but distinctive drum grooves and melodic synthesizer textures. The classic style became widely recognized in the 1990s. West Coast rap covers a wider range of sounds: G-Funk is one important branch of that musical tradition, not another name for all West Coast hip-hop.

Don Rashid is an independent Swiss hip-hop artist making music inspired by that tradition. His take combines bass-heavy rap with lowrider imagery, nighttime streets and a cinematic atmosphere. It is West Coast-inspired music created independently rather than a claim to be part of the original California G-Funk scene.
