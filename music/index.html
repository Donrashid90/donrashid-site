The Don Rashid sound

Don Rashid is an independent Swiss artist whose music draws on West Coast hip-hop. On Lowrider, deep bass and a cruising groove carry the night-drive mood. The two Westcoast singles are another way into that sound.

Watch Lowrider’s official visual or Westcoast on YouTube. Explore more official Don Rashid music videos and Shorts, including Mirage Code, Compton and Tyson Instinct.

African Analog takes the catalog toward Kenya’s Indian Ocean coast. For more releases and videos, visit Don Rashid on Spotify and the official YouTube channel.
