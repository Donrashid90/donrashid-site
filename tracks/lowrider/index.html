Inside the Lowrider sound

Lowrider connects Don Rashid's music to his broader visual world: West Coast-inspired grooves, analog textures, deep bass and the atmosphere of a late-night cruise. The track and its imagery reflect the artist's independently developed sound and aesthetic.

The music takes inspiration from classic lowrider and G-Funk culture, while keeping its own cinematic tone. Discover more tracks and albums in the Don Rashid catalog.
