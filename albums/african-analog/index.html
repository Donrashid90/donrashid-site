About African Analog

African Analog explores another side of the Don Rashid sound: influences associated with the African coast meet the lowrider atmosphere, weighty drums and cinematic approach of his independent releases. The record is part of the wider music and visual world built around the Don Rashid name.

Start with these tracks

01 / Malindi Nights

The opener takes its name from Malindi, a town on Kenya’s Indian Ocean coast. It gives the album’s coastal theme a specific place. Listen to Malindi Nights on Spotify.

02 / Indian Ocean

The second track takes its name from the ocean that borders Malindi. Together, the opening titles move from one coastal town to the wider region that frames the album. Listen to Indian Ocean on Spotify.
