Don Rashid / Artist biography

Who is Don Rashid?

Don Rashid is an independent hip-hop artist from Switzerland. His music moves between West Coast-inspired rap, G-Funk, heavy low-end, old-school drum grooves and cinematic night-drive atmosphere.

Rather than treating each track as a standalone release, Don Rashid connects music, videos, artwork and limited-edition merchandise through a recognizable visual language: lowriders, chrome, night streets and analog warmth.

Music with global influences

The artist's sound brings together West Coast and Southern hip-hop influences with a global perspective. His album African Analog explores another part of that vision, including tracks such as Malindi Nights and Indian Ocean. Lowrider reflects the driving G-Funk side of his music.
