Artist bio & artwork

Don Rashid is an independent hip-hop artist from Switzerland. His sound brings West Coast rap, G-Funk and lowrider culture into a cinematic setting, with heavy bass, old-school rhythms and visuals built around chrome, city lights and night drives. African Analog extends that sound toward Kenya’s Indian Ocean coast.
